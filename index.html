Our Mission & Values

To create an atmosphere of ‘iron sharpening iron’ of business ethics for ‘the welfare of the city’ in South McLennan County.

Also to have a great time making connections, doing good works, ‘loving God’ and our neighbors.

Proverbs 27:17Jeremiah 29:7Matthew 22:37–39

Sharpen

Honest peers who make each other better in business and in character.

Serve

Good works that seek the welfare of every town in South McLennan County.

Connect

Real relationships between neighbors, merchants, and makers — and a great time doing it.

Love

Loving God and our neighbors as the foundation of how we do business.

Who we are

About Us

Founder

Rob

A Salesforce consultant and business analyst with deep roots in the Bruceville-Eddy community, Rob founded the Chamber to bring business owners across South McLennan County together — to sharpen one another, serve the area, and build something that outlasts any one of us.

Marketing Director

Darrell

A Hillsdale College graduate with a background in marketing at the Texas Flyover, Darrell runs marketing and operations for the Chamber — keeping members informed and events running smoothly. He lives in Round Rock with his family, where he's usually found reading to his kids.

Board Member

Matt

Matt Teifke built a real estate business from the ground up into TR3 Capital, now managing $110M in assets. Inspired by Trammell Crow's generosity-first approach to business, he's always glad to talk shop with anyone, no matter their experience level.
